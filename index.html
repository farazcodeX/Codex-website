NEONBOOK / KNOWLEDGE IN MOTION

NeonBook

Learn. Test. Compete. Climb.

The idea

Read. Put your understanding to the test.

NeonBook is a startup launched by the Neon team, particularly Ilya BOTO. I contributed and remain on the team. The platform connects technology books with tests, scores, points and weekly rankings.

Stack: Node.js · HTML · CSS · JavaScript

Book

Choose a technology book and start learning.

Test

Take a 20-question test based on the book.

Score

Your answers determine your test score.

Points

Your results contribute to your overall points.

Competition

Weekly tests keep learning active and competitive.

Leaderboard

Your points determine your position among learners.

Your library. Your next step.
