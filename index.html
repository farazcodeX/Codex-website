THE IDEA

Learning becomes more engaging when knowledge can be measured.

NeonBook is a startup launched by the Neon team, particularly Ilya BOTO. The platform turns learning from technology books into a competitive experience through tests, scores, points, and rankings.

PRODUCT

Learn. Test. Compete. Climb.

Explore a curated collection of technology books, read at your own pace and build your skills step by step.

→
Book
Choose a technology book and start learning.

Test your knowledge with 20 carefully designed questions and see how much you've learned.

→
Test
Take a 20-question test based on the book.

Get an instant score based on your answers and track your progress over time.

→
Score
Your answers determine your test score.

Earn points for every test you complete and improve your rank as you progress.

→
Points
Your results contribute to your overall points.

Join the weekly challenge, compare your results with others and stay motivated.

→
Competition
Weekly tests keep the learning process active and competitive.

See where you stand, climb the ranks and become a top learner in the community.

→
Leaderboard
Your points determine your position among other learners.
